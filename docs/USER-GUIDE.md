# User guide

## Barangay Health Workers

1. Sign in using the BHW account type. If SMS 2FA is enabled, enter the code sent to your registered mobile.
2. Open Child Profiles to search by name/Child ID or filter by purok, sex, age, nutrition category, immunization status, or registration status.
3. Add a child, record guardian details, capture privacy consent, and attach available Birth Certificate, Residency Certificate, and Pink Card files. Missing documents may be uploaded later.
4. Use the child record links to enter immunizations, growth measurements, and check-up notes. Growth scoring requires official WHO tables imported by the administrator.
5. Review Pending Registrations and parent-uploaded documents. Verify entries or reject them with a correction reason.
6. Use Schedules to find upcoming and overdue doses. Select pending children to queue reminders for opted-in primary guardians; the scheduled worker sends them.
7. Open Graphical Reports for aggregate summaries and filter exports by date and Purok.

## Administrators

- Create staff and parent accounts in Users Management. Parent access uses a linked mobile number and SMS verification.
- Update center information, puroks, vaccine catalog, SMS behavior, security settings, audit records, and backups in System Settings.
- Store Semaphore credentials only in the environment file. Schedule the SMS and reminder commands.
- Confirm vaccine timing with the local clinical lead before clinical use.

## Parents and guardians

1. Ask the health center to create and link your account. Sign in using Parent or Guardian. New accounts use SMS verification and ask you to set a password.
2. Use the dashboard child switcher to review upcoming doses, growth history, recent notices, and opt into a day-before reminder. My Children lists records linked to your account; each profile shows immunization, growth, medical history, and document status.
3. Submit another child's registration in the portal. Staff must verify details and documents before a Child ID and schedule are issued.
4. Open Documents to view or download uploaded files, check their review status, or upload missing documents for any linked child. Staff review new uploads before verification.
5. Request an appointment and check its status in the portal.
6. Download the child record PDF when needed. Keep it private and share it only with an authorized care provider.
